Value against size

Each dot is a ranked protocol. The line is the fitted curve; the floor is the $50M watchlist floor. Big protocols are worth less per TVL dollar than small ones — that sub-linear slope is the whole point of fitting a curve instead of a flat multiple.

Method, measured honestly

Model. Over the 128 traded anchors, ln(market cap) = a + b × (ln TVL − centre), fitted with Huber weights so a few giant outliers cannot drag the curve. Growth, age without a token and chain count were each tested as extra terms and dropped: every one of them made the blind test worse. Category offsets were tested the same way and dropped too.

What “blind” means here. Leave-one-out refits the curve 128 times, each time predicting the one protocol left out. Walk-forward fits only the oldest 60% (by listing date) and predicts the newest 40%, then the other way round. Nothing in the table below was measured on the data it was fitted to.

Uncertainty. Each row carries a P25–P75 band from the leave-one-out residuals. Half of the real market caps fell inside that band, 81% inside the P10–P90 band. A verdict uses the middle of the band, never the top.

Farm plans. A 12-week plan: 16 transactions (2 to set up, 1 a week, 2 to exit) at the chain’s typical gas price, deposit sized by TVL ($100 / $250 / $500). The expected allocation is a scenario, not a promise: the base case assumes an allocation worth 0.001% of the implied value. Verdict: FARM if the base-case net clears $50, MARGINAL if it clears $0, SKIP otherwise. The sensitivity grid shows the same net under other drop-share and deposit assumptions.

Limits. This ranks protocols by scale; it does not predict a price, a launch date, or whether a token ever appears. “No token recorded” means the snapshot records none, not that none exists.
